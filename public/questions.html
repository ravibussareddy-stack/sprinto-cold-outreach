How questions work

  1. Facts are researched, not askedIf something is missing (a certificate, a security page), the system looks harder: sitemap, trust and privacy pages.
  2. Only judgement calls are asked"I found this, my rules don't say how to read it." For example: SOC 2 Type I only, a badge with no statement, an old report, an acquisition, a company already on Vanta or Drata, a company that sells compliance software itself.
  3. Asked once, applied everywhereEach situation is asked once; later companies with the same finding are added as examples. Your answer applies to every company from its next run.
  4. New situationsIf the system meets something its rules don't cover, it asks in its own words and you answer in yours (at most 5 waiting at a time).

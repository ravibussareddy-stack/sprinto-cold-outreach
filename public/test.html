Run a test case

Sends one company through the whole pipeline: scrape, qualify, contact, pain points, email.

Input

Off: draft only, nothing is sent. The API's own default is send: true.
Takes about 60-90 seconds.

Automated tests: POST /api/run_case with {"domain": "atlan.com", "contact": {"first_name": "Priya", "email": "priya@atlan.com", "title": "CTO"}, "send": false}. GET /api/run_case returns the format.

What you will get

  • The decision, and the score line by line
  • Certification evidence: quoted, with its source page
  • The contact, two pain points (fact and hypothesis)
  • The email, its draft checks and the critic's scores, plus the fact-check score
  • With send on: the email in our test inbox, with the offerings deck attached
